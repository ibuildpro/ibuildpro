## Johnathan Hyde

Software is a building people work inside every day. 🏢

Most people only see dashboards, approvals, reports, and customer records. They do not need to think about what is underneath. But I do.

Who gets into which room.<br>
Which doors need to stay locked.<br>
Where the wiring runs.<br>
Where the plumbing connects.<br>
And whether the foundation will still hold when the business gets bigger, busier, and more complicated.

That means working across architecture and implementation. I design the system, then build the application layers that support it: multi-tenant architecture, data models, APIs, role-based access, approval flows, reporting, billing, and audit trails.
